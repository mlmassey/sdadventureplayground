## What Is an Adventure Playground?

### A playground built by kids, not just for kids.

Adventure Playgrounds are a different kind of play environment. Originating in Denmark in the 1940s and embraced by communities around the world, they put children in charge of their own adventures.

Instead of manufactured playground equipment and carefully prescribed activities, children are given access to open space, reclaimed materials, tools, and the freedom to create.

They might:

- Build forts, towers, bridges, and imaginative structures using real wood, hammers, nails, and saws.
- Dig, experiment with water and mud, and explore the natural environment.
- Create art, invent games, and transform everyday materials into something extraordinary.
- Work together to solve problems, negotiate ideas, and tackle challenges.
- Take reasonable risks, make mistakes, and learn through experience.

Trained playworkers supervise the environment, support safe tool use, and step in when necessary — but the children lead the play.

**The magic isn't in what adults build for children. It's in what children discover they can build themselves.**

## Why San Diego Needs This

### Kids need more opportunities to be kids.

Children today have incredible access to technology, organized activities, and structured learning. But opportunities for independent, imaginative, hands-on play can be harder to find.

We believe there's something important about giving children a space where they can experiment without being told exactly what to do.

Where failure is part of the fun.

Where a crooked wooden fort is an accomplishment.

Where children learn to work together because they have something they want to build.

Adventure Playgrounds create opportunities to develop:

**Creativity & Imagination** — Turning ordinary materials into extraordinary ideas.

**Confidence & Independence** — Making decisions, taking initiative, and discovering personal capabilities.

**Problem-Solving & Resilience** — Experimenting, making mistakes, and finding solutions.

**Collaboration & Community** — Building friendships, sharing resources, and working toward common goals.

**Practical Skills** — Learning how to use tools, understand materials, and create things with their own hands.

We want to bring back some of the freedom, creativity, and adventure that made childhood exploration so memorable — while providing a supervised environment designed for today's communities.

## Inspired by Sacramento Adventure Playground

### A decade of imagination, creativity, and community.

We don't have to look far to see what's possible.

In August 2016, Sacramento Adventure Playground opened at the Maple Neighborhood Center in South Sacramento.

Operated by Fairytale Town, the program has spent the past decade providing children with opportunities for free, supervised, child-directed play.

Instead of conventional playground equipment, Sacramento provides children with real tools, reclaimed building materials, art supplies, and the freedom to create their own play spaces.

Its approach is simple: children learn and grow through experiences they create themselves.

The playground serves local youth through free after-school programming, community play days, and additional activities. Its continued operation demonstrates how a community-supported adventure playground can become a lasting neighborhood resource.

**Sacramento has shown that this idea isn't just an experiment. It's a model that can serve children and families year after year.**

Our goal is to learn from Sacramento's experience and bring a similar opportunity to San Diego County.

[Explore Sacramento Adventure Playground](https://sacadventureplay.wordpress.com/)

## Our Vision for San Diego

### More than a playground. A place that grows with its community.

We envision a permanent, year-round outdoor adventure playground where children have room to build, explore, and create.

A space with building materials, tools, open-ended play areas, and structures that evolve over time through the imagination and efforts of the children themselves.

The playground would be supported by trained staff who encourage independence while helping children understand tools, materials, and appropriate risks.

We want it to be welcoming, accessible, and affordable — ideally offering free community play opportunities.

Our vision could include:

- **Open Adventure Play:** Regular supervised sessions where children direct their own activities.
- **Community Play Days:** Opportunities for local families to discover adventure play together.
- **School & Youth Partnerships:** Programs connecting children with hands-on, creative experiences.
- **Camps & Special Programs:** Optional structured opportunities that help support ongoing operations.
- **Community Building:** Volunteer projects, donated materials, and partnerships with local organizations.

Our objective is not to build a high-priced attraction.

**It's to create something meaningful for the community that can sustain itself and serve generations of San Diego children.**

## Let's Build Something Together

### Seeking land, partnerships, and community support.

San Diego Adventure Playground is currently in the concept and partnership development stage.

We're exploring opportunities to establish a permanent home in San Diego County, particularly through partnerships with organizations that share our interest in children's development, education, recreation, and community life.

We believe an Adventure Playground could be a valuable addition to:

**New Residential Communities**

A distinctive recreational amenity that brings families together, encourages outdoor activity, and creates a lasting neighborhood gathering place.

**Parks & Community Spaces**

An innovative complement to traditional playgrounds, offering creative, hands-on experiences that conventional recreational facilities rarely provide.

**Educational & Cultural Organizations**

A natural extension of programs focused on childhood development, creativity, engineering, making, and experiential learning.

**Businesses & Community Sponsors**

An opportunity to support an accessible, community-focused program through funding, materials, equipment, expertise, or volunteer involvement.

### What We're Looking For

We're especially interested in connecting with:

- Landowners and developers interested in providing or leasing outdoor space.
- Community organizations, museums, schools, and recreation providers interested in partnerships.
- Local businesses willing to contribute building materials, tools, equipment, or financial support.
- Educators, playworkers, makers, and volunteers who share our enthusiasm for child-directed play.
- Families and community members who want to help bring this idea to life.

We're flexible about how a partnership could work, including land leases, shared community facilities, sponsorships, and collaborative operating arrangements.

Our priority is finding the right location and partners to create a safe, sustainable, community-serving playground.
